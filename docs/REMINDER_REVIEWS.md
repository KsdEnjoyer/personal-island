# Startup and half-hour reminder reviews

The frontend-ready handshake reveals a compact overview on every application start, including launches without `--show`. Existing wall-clock boundaries remain :00 and :30; the interval is not shifted by launch time.

For startup and scheduled reveals, unfinished tasks with a reminder time (or a deadline when no reminder exists) at or before the next 30 minutes appear in the same 272×56 island. Most recently missed reminders come first, then older missed reminders, then upcoming reminders chronologically. Delivery timestamps do not exclude unfinished work from a later review. Completed tasks and undated tasks are excluded. An empty review uses the usual default island. The manual hotkey continues to show the usual clock/music island.

The compact review retains the current time, open-task count and weather. Titles fit two lines; clicking opens and highlights the original task. The counter arrow cycles through all eligible tasks. Automatic cycling happens every 4.5 seconds, pauses under the cursor and allows an 8–15 second reading lease. Hovering keeps the island visible. Track-change popups do not replace an active review; the hotkey can close it normally.

Punctual bell notifications still handle reminders that become due after application launch. Earlier reminders go through reviews instead of a startup queue of old bells. The SQLite cutoff is applied before the notification query's limit so a large historical backlog cannot starve fresh alerts. Nothing is completed or deleted by a review. Only an exact due reminder actually painted in the review can be marked presented; future reminders are not acknowledged. Native checks require a visible compact review and its current generation. The recurring review remains independent of this one-shot delivery flag.

Validation: task-core tests use temporary databases to check backlog filtering and exact-version acknowledgement. `qa-reminder-review-ui.py` covers a 13:01 launch with a missed 13:00 reminder, previously delivered tasks, upcoming tasks, completion, half-hour review, task navigation and default/manual behavior. `qa-reminder-review-native.py` checks real packaged startup, an accelerated real scheduler and restart persistence with an isolated database and WebView profile. Existing reminder choreography and shortcut regression suites remain applicable.
